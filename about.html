BMGT407 - INFORMATION SYSTEMS PROJECTS
BMGT407 is the capstone course for Information Systems (IS) majors. Through a complete IS development process, students design and implement functional prototypes that solve practical business challenges. Group 1 is completing a project about Kung Fu Tea’s inventory system.
